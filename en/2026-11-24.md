## A Pinch

Phatic communication. A concept named by the anthropologist Bronisław Malinowski in 1923: the work words do to keep a relationship going by the act of talking itself, rather than by the information they carry. Talk about the weather, and greetings, are the standard examples. In the words of today's talk: small talk may be thin as an exchange of information, but it does the work of holding a place together. The problem was never the thinness itself, but what one can bring into that place.
